# DFG Modules, Rates and Budget Justification

Sources: forms 50.01 (03/25), 52.01 (09/24), 52.02 (03/25), 52.06 (09/22), 55.04 (01/26),
60.12 (01/26). Verify current versions before submission — rates change annually.

---

## 1. Module system

A module can only be requested within a programme that offers it. For Research Grants, at
least one of the first four below must be requested; the remainder are added alongside.

| Module | Form | Purpose |
|--------|------|---------|
| Basic Module | 52.01 | Direct project costs, project-specific staff, instrumentation, publication allowance |
| Temporary Positions for PIs | 52.02 | The applicant's own position |
| Replacements | 52.03 | Release from teaching or administrative duties |
| Temporary Substitutes for Clinicians | 52.04 | Release from patient care |
| Mercator Fellows | 52.05 | Intensive, long-term exchange with researchers in Germany or abroad |
| Project-Specific Workshops | 52.06 | Workshops and colloquia; cannot be requested alone |
| Public Relations | 52.07 | Presenting the work to a lay public; cannot be requested alone |
| Standard Allowance for Equity and Diversity | 52.14 | Up to €1,000 per funding year |

## 2. Personnel — 2026 standard rates (form 60.12, 01/26)

Staff are funded at **standard amounts** based on full-time employment and gross employer
costs, including social security and the annual special payment. They are not intended for
calculating individual remuneration, and do not replace the employer's pay-scale assessment.

| Category | €/year | €/month | Pay-scale orientation |
|----------|--------|---------|----------------------|
| Professorship | 123,600 | 10,300 | W |
| Emmy Noether group leader / Heisenberg | 102,300 | 8,525 | E14 L5 – E15 L4 |
| Medical research assistant | 111,000 | 9,250 | Ä1 L2 – Ä2 L1 |
| Postdoctoral researcher or comparable | 88,200 | 7,350 | E13 L3 – E14 L2 |
| Doctoral researcher or comparable | 81,600 | 6,800 | E13 L2 – E14 L1 |
| Other research assistant (bachelor's) | 67,200 | 5,600 | E9 – E12 |
| Non-academic staff member | 60,000 | 5,000 | E2 L1 – E9 L2 |
| Student/research assistants | as required | — | local customary rates |

**Category definitions.** *Postdoctoral researcher* requires a doctorate, or ≥3 years'
professional experience with a university/FH diploma or master's. *Doctoral researcher*
covers those without a doctorate, generally ≥50% positions, or staff not pursuing a
doctorate with <3 years' experience. Projects limited to producing a thesis are **not**
eligible for DFG funding.

**What to state for each position:** duration of employment (generally matching project
duration), the category, and the working hours as a percentage of the standard week. In the
project description, briefly explain each person's tasks, matching the work programme, and
give the intended start date for DFG payment.

**Family leave.** If a project head reduces hours, or a research employee temporarily drops
out, for childcare or dependent care, a supplementary proposal may be submitted to keep the
project on track.

## 3. Basic Module — direct project costs

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| 2.1 Equipment ≤€10,000, software, consumables | List instruments individually with prices; justify each software item; give amounts per consumable subcategory |
| 2.2 Travel | Justify each type (collaboration, fieldwork, archives, conferences); for partners abroad, give number and duration of trips; for stays >1 month, name and address the partner |
| 2.3 Visiting researchers | Transportation and maintenance; honoraria only exceptionally |
| 2.4 Experimental animals | Purchase, breeding, keeping |
| 2.5 Other | Third-party contracts, participant compensation, documentation services, vehicle rental, **user fees for major instrumentation and core facilities** |
| 2.6 Publication expenses | Up to **€750/year**; up to **€5,000/year** for justified high-cost book formats; larger book projects apply separately (form 51.10) |

**Instrumentation >€10,000 per item** goes in the separate instrumentation section, split
into ≥€10,000 and ≥€50,000. Installation and operating costs cannot be covered by DFG — the
host institution must confirm in writing that it will cover them.

### Not fundable

Own salary as PI (use module 52.02); secretarial services; construction, fittings,
furnishings, rent; general infrastructure (office furniture, tools, work clothes), office
materials, postage, telecommunications; fees for using the university's computing
facilities; instruments constituting core support in the subject area, **including workplace
computers**; upgrades or repair of instruments not originally DFG-financed; deductible VAT.

## 4. Core facilities and usage costs (form 55.04)

Usage fees for major instrumentation and core facilities are requested under Basic Module
item 2.5 — DFG covers only costs specifically required for the project, while basic funding
of the facility must come from institutional core support.

Form 55.04 sets usage flat rates for flow cytometry, electron microscopy, light microscopy,
**magnetic resonance imaging**, mass spectrometry and NMR spectroscopy. For imaging-heavy
projects, check the applicable flat rate rather than inventing a per-hour figure, and state
the number of sessions or hours the work programme requires.

## 5. Temporary Positions for Principal Investigators (52.02)

Funded at the postdoctoral rate. Key conditions:

- Generally **full-time**; part-time (≥50%) only exceptionally, for family reasons,
  disability or chronic illness. Apply as full-time and reduce later if awarded, notifying
  DFG in advance.
- **Two weekly teaching hours** per semester are permitted within working time.
- International research visits generally limited to **one-third** of project duration.
- Requires an **employer statement (form 41.027)** confirming the institution will assume
  employer responsibilities and provide the necessary basic infrastructure.
- Any existing employment must be **suspended** for the funding period, with proof of leave.
- **Tenured and junior professors in Germany are not eligible.**
- May apply after submitting the thesis (with thesis, proof of submission, and supervisor's
  statement), but funding starts only once the doctorate is complete.
- While a proposal for this module is pending, the applicant **cannot** submit another for
  it, nor proposals under Walter Benjamin, Emmy Noether or Heisenberg.
- Duty-to-cooperate rules (55.01) apply at Max Planck, Fraunhofer, Helmholtz and Leibniz
  institutions and international facilities in Germany.

## 6. Justification patterns

Reviewers check that funds and work programme imply each other in both directions. Each item
should answer: *what is it for, why this size, and which work package needs it?*

**Personnel.** State role, category, percentage, months, the work packages covered, and why
the expertise is not already available.

> "One doctoral researcher (100%, 36 months) will run WP1 and WP3. WP1 requires 8 months of
> data collection at 30 sessions per month; WP3 adds 14 months of acquisition plus 6 months
> of analysis. The remaining 8 months cover setup, piloting and manuscript preparation."

**Consumables.** Derive from the design, not from a round number.

> "Consumables are calculated from 130 participants × [€X] per session (electrodes, gel,
> disposables), plus [€Y] for replacement coils over the funding period."

**Equipment.** State why it is project-specific and not core support, and confirm
institutional coverage of running costs.

**Travel.** Name destinations and purposes rather than requesting a lump sum.

**Publication costs.** Anchor to the expected number of open-access articles and the
€750/year ceiling.

A useful final pass: for every euro requested, point to the sentence in section 2.3 that
requires it; for every work package, point to the budget line that resources it. Anything
unmatched in either direction will be questioned.
